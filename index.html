One-Stop Entertainment

Every mode of play under one electrifying roof. Walk in and pick what you are in the mood for.

PC Gaming

RTX 4070 Super & 240Hz monitors

From Valorant to Dota, every machine runs a 4070 Super behind a 240Hz panel. Logitech mechanical boards, tuned mouse pads, no input lag, no excuses.

PS5 Gaming

50+ titles across genres

Fifty-plus current-gen titles, played solo or in a private room with your squad. Couch, big screen, no one kicking you out at match point.

VR Gaming

Immersive virtual worlds

Headset on, room gone. Battle in futuristic arenas or walk through storylines that do not fit on a flat screen.

Car Simulator

Full rig, no licence needed

State-of-the-art motion rig, force feedback, screen that wraps around you. Enthusiast or first-timer, you do not need a licence.

Pool

Relaxing pool sessions

Chalk up for a frame or master the table properly. The good way to cool down after a round that went badly.

Board Games

Classic strategy & fun

Timeless board games for strategy, table talk and laughter. Works just as well with four people and no screens.

Cafe Dining

Play, eat, repeat

American and Chinese cuisine, starters and milkshakes. Order mid-session and keep the game running.

Lounge & Darts

Slow games, cold drinks

Cafe seating away from the screens when you want to just talk, plus darts and nothing at all for a bit.
